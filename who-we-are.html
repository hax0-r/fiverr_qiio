Who we are

With headquarters in Zurich, Switzerland, qiio is at the forefront of Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) solutions. We develop and deploy state-of-the-art, edge-to-cloud IIoT systems that enable businesses from a wide range of industries to increase their efficiency, lower costs, reduce field activities and increase profits.

We are a team of talented developers and experienced managers with strong backgrounds in hardware, embedded software, security, cloud computing and business. The combination of extended practical and academic expertise allows us to deliver revolutionary technological solutions to global businesses, especially in hard-to-reach places.
