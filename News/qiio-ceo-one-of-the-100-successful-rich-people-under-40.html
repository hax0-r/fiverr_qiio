qiio CEO, one of the 100 successful rich people under 40

Felix Adamczyk, the CEO of qiio, was nominated by BILANZ as one of the 100 successful rich people under 40.
About three-quarters of the BILANZ 100 (successful) rich under the age of 40 are young entrepreneurs. qiio is honored that the CEO, Felix Adamczyk, is one of the nominees.
