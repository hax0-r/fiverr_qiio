December 15, 2020

qiio’s Concentrator XN nominated as “Product Of the Year” by the renowned German ELEKTRONIK magazine, of the WEKA publishing house.

The qiio team is excited and honored to be one of the ten nominees in the category Embedded-System-Design.

The Product of the Year has been a sought-after prestigious prize for 23 consecutive years and is awarded in ten categories.

The winners are chosen by ELEKTRONIK’s competent tech-savvy readership.
