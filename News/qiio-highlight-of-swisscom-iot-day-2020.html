qiio highlight of Swisscom IoT Day 2020

More than 450 visitors and partners at Swisscom IoT Day 2020
Swisscom IoT Day 2020 took place on 23rd January 2020. More than 450 people gathered on 23 January 2020 at Hallenstadion, exchanging views and discussing the latest topics from the world of IoT in order to drive forward innovations together – in Switzerland and worldwide.

During the keynote “Unlocking IoT’s Potential”, Tony Shakib (IoT Business Acceleration Leader, Microsoft Azure) introduced qiio and its IoT solution.

IoT-enabled exhibition booth
qiio participated in the exhibition and showcased an IoT solution for a smart booth. The demo set monitors the real-time ambient data, such as temperature, heat-flux, and humidity. The cloud portal will show the collected data. In addition, it is easy and quick to compile a report that is compliant to ISO standards.
