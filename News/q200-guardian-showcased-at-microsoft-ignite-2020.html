q200 Guardian showcased at Microsoft Ignite 2020

q200 Guardian, which provides all the interfaces exposed by the qiio Concentrator XN, was showcased by Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella at Microsoft Ignite 2020.

Microsoft Ignite is for anyone who is motivated to be on the frontier of innovation and tech. Satya Nadella, the CEO of Microsoft, discussed how we’re innovating across the tech stack to help organizations–big and small–address real-world challenges.

In his session, Satya Nadella presented q200 Guardian, a new Guardian device built on Azure Sphere to securely connect devices via cellular bypassing the need for WiFi. q200 Guardian will be distributed through AT&T to customers and enable a highly secure cellular solution for their devices.

q200 Guardian is now available and open for order. For more information, please check our product page.
