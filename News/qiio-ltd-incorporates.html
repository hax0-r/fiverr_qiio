September 26, 2019

qiio Ltd Incorporates

We are excited to announce the next phase in the growth of our company: we are now officially incorporated as a privately-held joint-stock company! With immediate effect, our company name becomes qiio Ltd in English, qiio AG in German and qiio SA in French.

With incorporation, we increase our capital reserves, give our company the autonomy to exist as an independent entity, and provide liquidity for our shareholders. In effect, it strengthens the foundation of our firm enabling us to achieve the next level of expansion!
