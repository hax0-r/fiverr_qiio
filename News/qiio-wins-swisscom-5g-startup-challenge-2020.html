December 17, 2020

qiio wins Swisscom 5G StartUp Challenge 2020

Zurich-based start-up qiio convinced the eleven-member jury with “Smart Connectivity” and was one of five winners to receive the esteemed award.

The Zurich start-up and ETH spin-off qiio (pronounced: «kio»), specializing in edge-to-cloud solutions for industrial IoT (Internet of Things), prevailed against 125 participants from thirty countries and won – as one of five companies – the Swisscom StartUp Challenge.
