December 15, 2020

Swisscom StartUp Challenge 2020

Swisscom announced the 10 finalists for the 5G Swisscom Startup Challenge 2020 and qiio is proud to be among them.

The finalists will present their 5G business case on Thursday, December 17th in front of a jury of ten. The Challenge will be hosted on a live YouTube stream for everybody to watch.

The jury includes members of the Swisscom Group Executive Board and representatives from Swisscom Ventures and program partners as well as Venturelab, Ericsson and Qualcomm.
