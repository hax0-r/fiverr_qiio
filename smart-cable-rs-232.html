Smart Cable RS-232

For point-to-point communications: connect your IoT asset to the qiio q200 and Development Kits using RS-232

Features

  • Supports qiio q200 Guardian and Development Kits: provides an industry standard RS-232 transceiver interface from your IoT asset to the ISU port (UART) of the q200 Guardian, Development Kit q200 or PoC in a Box Package. Converts CMOS UART levels to RS-232 levels and vice-versa. Maximum speed: 232 kbps.
  • Simple interface for point-to-point communications over short distances
  • Form factor & Interfaces: PCB with mikroBUS connectors and RS-232 cable adapter (SUB-D 9-pin connector)
  • Supply voltage: 3.3V
  • One of a wide range of Smart Cables from qiio: with the operator-certified q200 Guardian as the base design, use your preferred Smart Cable(s) to connect to different IoT assets without requiring re-certification
