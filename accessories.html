Choose how your IoT assets connect to the edge with qiio Smart Cables

Connect your IoT assets to the qiio q200 and Development Kits using your choice of industry standard protocols

Features

  • Comprehensive connectivity to your IoT assets: qiio lets you choose from a complete range of Smart Cables to connect your fixed and mobile assets to the IoT edge
  • Supports qiio q200 Guardian and Development Kits: easily connects from the provided ISU (I2C, SPI, UART) port to your IoT asset
  • Use the operator-certified q200 Guardian as the base design, then use your preferred Smart Cable to connect to a variety of IoT assets without requiring re-certification
  • A complete range of industry-standard wired protocols including RS-232 / RS-422 / RS-485 and Ethernet
  • Smart Cable CAN provides direct interfacing to a vehicle bus for communicating with mobile IoT assets
  • Smart Cable WiFi/BLE ESP32 provides a Bluetooth Low Energy 5.0 and WiFi interface to the ISU port of q200 Guardian or Development Kits
  • Smart Cable BLE NRF52 provides a Bluetooth Low Energy 5.2 interface to the ISU port of q200 Guardian or Development Kits
  • Smart Cable USB Voltage Booster provides a clean and stable USB power source for the q200 Guardian in environments where USB power source is unstable
  • Smart Cable Storage SDCard lets you add memory extension via the ISU port of the q200 Guardian or Development Kits
