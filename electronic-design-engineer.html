Electronic Design Engineer (100% Switzerland)

Description

We are looking for a an Electronic Design Engineer to join qiio’s Swiss team in Zurich. We want you do create the future of ultra secure cellular IoT edge devices. You will be working with an experience team from design to manufacturing. Your work will be primarily in Switzerland and in the hardware lab in Zurich. Whenever needed you will cooperate with the qiio factory team in Germany. As qiio is an international startup with an international customer base there are opportunities to travel when you are open for that and if it adds value.

Duties and responsibilities

Your tasks will include the following:

  • Work on the hardware design of ultra secure cellular IoT edge devices. The designs are ARM based on go from low performance ARM micro-controller architectures to high performance ARM multi-core architectures
  • Work together with the qiio factory team on ultra reliable manufacturing at automotive grade
  • Lead the hardware lab in Zurich with a machine park for different types of prototyping

Skills/Knowledge required

The ideal candidate will have:

  • Degree in Electrical Engineering ETH / EPFL or similar
  • Experience in high speed signal designs (RAM, Flash, USB)
  • Experience in impedance controlled designs (700MHz – 5GHz)
  • Experience in power management designs
  • Experience in thermal management
  • Experience in working with Altium Designer
  • Hardware prototyping skills to lead the Zurich lab (soldering, 3D printing, laser cutting)
  • Optional: CAD design experience for housing
